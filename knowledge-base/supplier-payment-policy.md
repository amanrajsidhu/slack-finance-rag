# Supplier Payment Policy — Hartwell Building Supplies Ltd

**Version:** 3.1  
**Owner:** Finance Director  
**Last reviewed:** January 2026  
**Applies to:** All staff with purchasing authority

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out Hartwell Building Supplies Ltd's approach to supplier payments, invoice approval, and purchase authorisation. All staff involved in purchasing or finance must follow this policy. Failure to follow the approval process may result in invoices being rejected and payments being delayed.

---

## 2. Standard Payment Terms

Hartwell's standard payment terms with suppliers are **30 days from the date of invoice** unless a different term has been agreed in writing and approved by the Finance Director.

- New suppliers: payment terms must be agreed before a purchase order is raised
- Preferred suppliers (on the approved supplier list): standard 30-day terms apply unless otherwise noted on the supplier record in Xero
- Suppliers requesting early payment must submit a written request to the Finance Manager — early payment discounts of 2% or more will be considered

Payment runs are processed every **two weeks**, on the first and third Friday of each month. Invoices must be approved and posted in Xero by the Wednesday preceding a payment run to be included in that run.

---

## 3. Invoice Approval Thresholds

All supplier invoices must be approved before payment is processed. Approval authority is based on the net invoice value (excluding VAT).

| Invoice Value (net) | Approval Authority |
|---------------------|--------------------|
| Up to £1,000 | Self-approve (budget holder or requisitioner) |
| £1,001 to £5,000 | Line manager approval required |
| £5,001 to £15,000 | Finance Manager approval required |
| £15,001 to £50,000 | Finance Director approval required |
| Over £50,000 | Finance Director and Managing Director joint approval required |

Approval must be confirmed in writing (email is acceptable) before the invoice is posted in Xero. Verbal approvals are not accepted.

---

## 4. Purchase Orders

A purchase order (PO) must be raised in advance for any purchase over £500. Invoices received without a corresponding PO number will be returned to the supplier and will not be processed until a PO is raised and approved.

POs are raised in Xero by the requisitioning budget holder. The PO must include:
- Supplier name and contact details
- Description of goods or services
- Net value and expected delivery date
- Budget code and cost centre

Once raised, POs require the same approval as invoices based on value (see Section 3).

---

## 5. New Supplier Onboarding

Before a purchase order can be raised with a new supplier, the following must be completed:

1. New Supplier Request Form submitted to the Finance team (form available on the shared drive)
2. Supplier bank details confirmed via a direct callback to the supplier — do not use bank details provided by email without verbal verification
3. Supplier added to the approved supplier list in Xero by the Finance Manager
4. Credit check completed for any supplier where anticipated annual spend exceeds £20,000

New supplier onboarding typically takes 3-5 working days. Plan purchases accordingly.

---

## 6. Disputed Invoices

If an invoice is disputed:

1. The budget holder must notify the Finance team in writing within 5 working days of receipt
2. The Finance team will place a payment hold on the invoice in Xero and notify the supplier
3. The budget holder is responsible for resolving the dispute directly with the supplier
4. Once resolved, the budget holder must confirm in writing to the Finance team before payment is released
5. Disputed invoices outstanding for more than 30 days must be escalated to the Finance Manager

---

## 7. Expense Claims

Staff expense claims are processed monthly. Claims must be submitted by the 20th of the month to be included in that month's payment run. Claims submitted after the 20th will be processed in the following month.

All claims must be submitted via the expense claim form (available on the shared drive) and include original receipts. Claims over £250 require line manager approval before submission to Finance.

---

## 8. Fraud Prevention

- Never make a payment based solely on an instruction received by email, even if the email appears to come from a known supplier or internal colleague
- All requests to change supplier bank details must be verified by telephone using a number already held on the supplier record — not a number provided in the change request
- Any suspicious payment requests must be reported immediately to the Finance Manager or Finance Director
- Payments outside of the standard payment run require Finance Director approval regardless of value
