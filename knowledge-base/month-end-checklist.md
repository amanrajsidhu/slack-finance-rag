# Month-End Close Checklist — Hartwell Building Supplies Ltd

**Finance Team Use Only**  
**Checklist owner:** Finance Manager  
**Deadline:** All tasks must be completed by close of business on the 5th working day of the following month.

---

## Week 1: Cut-Off and Data Integrity

- [ ] Confirm sales order cut-off with the operations team — all orders dispatched before month-end must be invoiced in the current period
- [ ] Confirm purchase order cut-off — all goods received before month-end must have a corresponding purchase invoice or accrual raised
- [ ] Lock the sales ledger for the closed month in Xero once cut-off is confirmed
- [ ] Lock the purchase ledger once all supplier invoices for the period have been posted or accrued
- [ ] Confirm with the warehouse manager that all goods received notes (GRNs) for the month have been matched to purchase invoices or flagged for accrual

---

## Bank Reconciliation

- [ ] Download the month-end bank statement for the main current account (Lloyds Bank, account ending 4471)
- [ ] Download the month-end statement for the supplier payment account (Lloyds Bank, account ending 8823)
- [ ] Reconcile both accounts in Xero — all transactions matched, no unreconciled items older than 3 days
- [ ] Investigate and resolve any reconciling items before the pack is submitted
- [ ] Confirm petty cash balance with the office manager and post any outstanding expenses

---

## Accounts Receivable

- [ ] Run the aged debtors report in Xero as at month-end
- [ ] Confirm all invoices raised in the month have been sent to customers
- [ ] Flag any invoices over 60 days outstanding to the Finance Manager for review
- [ ] Post any credit notes approved during the month
- [ ] Confirm any prepayments received from customers are correctly allocated

---

## Accounts Payable

- [ ] Run the aged creditors report in Xero as at month-end
- [ ] Confirm all supplier invoices received before the cut-off have been posted
- [ ] Raise accruals for any goods received but not yet invoiced (GRNs without a matched purchase invoice)
- [ ] Confirm the next scheduled payment run date with the Finance Manager
- [ ] Post any supplier credit notes received during the month

---

## Accruals and Prepayments

- [ ] Review the recurring accruals schedule and post all standard monthly accruals (utilities, insurance, maintenance contracts)
- [ ] Post prepayment journals for any annual or quarterly costs paid in advance — spread over the remaining months of the contract
- [ ] Confirm depreciation journals have been posted for all fixed assets on the register
- [ ] Review payroll accrual — confirm the final payroll figure for the month and post the accrual if payroll has not yet been processed

---

## Payroll

- [ ] Confirm payroll has been processed and the correct net pay figure is posted to the bank
- [ ] Post payroll journals to the nominal ledger (gross wages, PAYE, NI, pension contributions)
- [ ] Confirm PAYE and NI liabilities are posted correctly as balance sheet creditors
- [ ] Confirm pension contributions have been submitted to the pension provider

---

## Fixed Assets

- [ ] Confirm no new assets were purchased in the month — if yes, add to the fixed asset register with correct depreciation rate and start date
- [ ] Confirm no assets were disposed of in the month — if yes, remove from the register and post the disposal journal
- [ ] Post monthly depreciation run

---

## Management Accounts Pack

- [ ] Run the profit and loss report for the month and year to date in Xero
- [ ] Run the balance sheet as at month-end
- [ ] Prepare the management accounts pack using the standard template (saved in the Finance shared drive)
- [ ] Include commentary on any variances greater than 10% versus budget
- [ ] Submit the completed pack to the Finance Director for review no later than the 4th working day of the following month
- [ ] Finance Director sign-off required before the pack is distributed to the board

---
