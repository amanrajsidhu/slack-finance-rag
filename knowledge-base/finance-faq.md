# Finance FAQ — Hartwell Building Supplies Ltd

**Maintained by:** Finance Team  
**Last updated:** January 2026  

This document answers the most common finance queries from staff across the business. If your question is not covered here, contact the Finance team directly.

---

## Invoices and Payments

**Q: Who approves invoices over £5,000?**  
A: Invoices between £5,001 and £15,000 require Finance Manager approval (James Okafor). Invoices between £15,001 and £50,000 require Finance Director approval (Sarah Hartwell). Invoices over £50,000 require joint approval from both the Finance Director and the Managing Director. Approval must be confirmed in writing before the invoice is posted.

**Q: What is the approval threshold for self-approving a purchase?**  
A: Any invoice or purchase up to £1,000 net can be self-approved by the budget holder or requisitioner. Anything above £1,000 requires line manager approval as a minimum.

**Q: When are payment runs processed?**  
A: Payment runs are processed every two weeks, on the first and third Friday of each month. To be included in a payment run, invoices must be approved and posted in Xero by the Wednesday before that Friday.

**Q: What are our standard payment terms with suppliers?**  
A: Standard payment terms are 30 days from the date of invoice. Different terms must be agreed in writing and approved by the Finance Director before a purchase order is raised.

**Q: A supplier is asking to be paid early. What do I do?**  
A: Direct the supplier to submit a written request to the Finance Manager. Early payment may be considered if the supplier is offering a discount of 2% or more. The Finance Manager will assess and respond.

**Q: I received an email asking me to update a supplier's bank details. What should I do?**  
A: Do not action this based on the email alone. All requests to change supplier bank details must be verified by calling the supplier on a number already held on their record in Xero — not a number provided in the email or change request. Report any suspicious requests to the Finance Manager immediately.

## Purchase Orders

**Q: Do I need a purchase order before buying something?**  
A: Yes, for any purchase over £500 a purchase order must be raised in Xero before the order is placed. Invoices received without a PO number will be returned to the supplier and will not be paid until a PO is raised.

**Q: How do I raise a purchase order?**  
A: Log into Xero, go to Purchases, and select New Purchase Order. Complete the supplier details, description, value, expected delivery date, and budget code. Submit for approval based on the value threshold in the payment policy. Contact the Finance team if you need help with the correct budget code.

**Q: How long does it take to set up a new supplier?**  
A: New supplier onboarding takes 3 to 5 working days. A New Supplier Request Form must be submitted to the Finance team first — the form is available on the shared drive. Do not place an order with a new supplier before they are set up in Xero.

**Q: What information do I need to set up a new supplier?**  
A: You need the supplier's full legal name, registered address, contact details, bank details (confirmed by direct telephone callback — not email), and VAT number if applicable. For any supplier where anticipated annual spend exceeds £20,000, a credit check is also required.

## Expenses

**Q: When do I need to submit my expense claim to be paid this month?**  
A: Expense claims must be submitted by the 20th of the month to be included in that month's payment run. Claims submitted after the 20th will be processed the following month.

**Q: My expense claim is over £250. Does it need approval?**  
A: Yes. Any expense claim over £250 requires line manager approval before it is submitted to the Finance team.

**Q: Where is the expense claim form?**  
A: The expense claim form is saved on the Finance shared drive under Templates > Expense Claim Form. All claims must include original receipts.

## Month-End

**Q: When does the month-end pack need to be submitted?**  
A: The Finance Manager must submit the completed management accounts pack to the Finance Director for review by the 4th working day of the following month. The Finance Director must sign off by the 5th working day before the pack is distributed to the board.

**Q: When is the purchase ledger locked at month-end?**  
A: The purchase ledger is locked once all supplier invoices for the period have been posted or accrued. The cut-off is confirmed with the operations team before the ledger is closed. Contact the Finance Manager if you need to post an invoice after the ledger has been locked.

**Q: I received a supplier invoice after month-end for goods delivered before month-end. What happens?**  
A: If goods were received before month-end, an accrual should have been raised. Pass the invoice to the Finance team and they will match it against the accrual in the new period. Do not post it to the prior month once the ledger is locked.

**Q: What is the bank reconciliation deadline?**  
A: Bank reconciliation for both accounts (Lloyds 4471 and Lloyds 8823) must be completed by the 2nd working day of the following month. This is the Finance Assistant's responsibility.

## VAT

**Q: What is our VAT number?**  
A: Hartwell Building Supplies Ltd is VAT registered. VAT number: GB 342 8819 04. Our VAT return period is quarterly.

**Q: A supplier has not included VAT on their invoice. What do I do?**  
A: Check whether the supplier is VAT registered. If they are, request a corrected invoice with VAT shown separately before processing payment. If you are unsure, contact the Finance team before posting.

**Q: Can I reclaim VAT on client entertainment?**  
A: No. VAT on client entertainment is not recoverable. Code client entertainment to account 7080 and ensure the VAT is posted as non-reclaimable when entering the invoice in Xero.
